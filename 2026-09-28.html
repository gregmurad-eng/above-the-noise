"The capability overhang, the gap between what these models can do and what almost anyone is using them with them, is an opportunity."

— Ethan Mollick, oneusefulthing.org, 18 September 2026

Mollick's argument in the piece is that current frontier models are already capable of doing work the vast majority of their users are not yet asking them to do, and that the gap between those two things — the "overhang" — is where the near-term returns from AI actually live. He names the four human levers that close it: deep knowledge in a specific domain, wide knowledge across domains, taste in selecting and refining outputs, and the agency to test the boundary. It is worth re-reading this week because the DC noise around Anthropic and the DevDay noise around GPT-6 Cyber both pull attention toward capability at the top of the curve, when the operator's leverage this quarter is at the bottom — the workflows already inside the box that no one has finished wiring up.
