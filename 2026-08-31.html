Lead Story

The EU designates ChatGPT as a Very Large Online Search Engine — the first AI chatbot under the DSA's strictest tier — Euronews

The European Commission today designated OpenAI's ChatGPT as a Very Large Online Search Engine (VLOSE) under the Digital Services Act, and Reddit and Roblox as Very Large Online Platforms (VLOPs), bringing the total number of services under the DSA's strictest compliance tier to 28.[1][2] The trigger is the 45-million-EU-monthly-user threshold: OpenAI's own disclosure put ChatGPT Search at roughly 159.1 million EU monthly active users for the six months ending March 31, 2026, well over triple the line.[1][3] Henna Virkkunen, the Commission's executive vice-president for tech sovereignty, security and democracy, put the meaning plainly: "These new designations mean that ChatGPT, Reddit and Roblox will now be held to a higher standard of scrutiny and accountability in the European Union."[1]

The obligations that clock starts are concrete and dated. Within four months — by November 30, 2026 — the three services must stand up annual systemic-risk assessments across illegal content, minor protection, user wellbeing, fundamental rights, electoral processes and public security; commission independent audits; share data with regulators and vetted researchers; and expose ad-transparency and content-moderation reporting.[1][2] Supervision is split: Ireland's Coimisiún na Meán is the digital-services coordinator for ChatGPT and Reddit; the Netherlands' Authority for Consumers and Markets takes Roblox.[1] Non-compliance can attract fines of up to 6% of global annual turnover, on top of the EU AI Act's separate 3% ceiling that already kicked in on August 2.[3]

The classification choice — VLOSE rather than VLOP — is the load-bearing move. Brussels is asserting that a general-purpose chatbot returning links, citations and generated summaries functions as a search engine for regulatory purposes; that legal category, first designed for Google Search and Bing, now covers a stateless model call. Every other frontier chatbot approaching the 45M EU-MAU line — Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, Grok, Meta AI — has just been shown the mechanism that will be used on it.[1][2] Verfassungsblog's academic argument for this exact classification landed months ago;[4] the Commission has now adopted it as the operative theory of the case.

The compounding pressure is what matters for anyone building on these models. The DSA layer stacks on top of the AI Act layer, and this week the FSB's chair separately warned G20 finance ministers that frontier-AI autonomy is now a category of systemic risk.[5] Two of the world's most senior regulatory addresses — EU DSA on the product, FSB on the balance sheet — landed inside 24 hours. The era in which "we're an API vendor, not a platform" was a defensible posture in Brussels is over. The systemic-risk assessment that ChatGPT must file by November 30 is the template every other frontier chatbot's compliance team will be reading in December.

So what: if you build on the OpenAI API for EU end users, treat November 30 as a real gate — ChatGPT's forced systemic-risk assessment will surface disclosure obligations (model-output logging, minor-protection controls, researcher data access) that flow downstream to your product, and your legal team wants the draft assessment in October, not on the deadline.

The Briefs

Anthropic warns infostealer malware is hijacking Claude sessions to drain usage — BleepingComputer

Anthropic is signing affected Claude users out, revoking sessions, wiping saved payment methods and refunding unauthorised charges after infostealer malware — Vidar, LummaC2, StealC, RedLine and Acreed on Windows, Atomic Stealer (AMOS) on macOS — harvested authenticated browser sessions and reused them to consume Claude usage limits without triggering 2FA.[6] Anthropic's own line to affected users: "We have no reason to believe that this malware is related to Claude, installed through Claude, or related to anything you did with Claude."[6]

So what: if your agents authenticate to Claude via a browser session on a shared or engineer-owned laptop, move to short-lived API keys with per-workload scoping this week — a stolen session cookie now spends your rate limit and your card, and MFA on the login does not stop it.

Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell file multi-billion-dollar copyright suit against Anthropic — Music Business Worldwide

Filed August 28 in the US District Court for the Northern District of California, the complaint names Anthropic, Dario Amodei and Benjamin Mann as defendants over the alleged use of "tens of thousands" of compositions — via Library Genesis / Pirate Library Mirror torrents, scraped licensed-lyrics sites, and Books3 / Common Crawl — and asks for up to $150,000 per work for wilful infringement plus $25,000 per removal of copyright management information.[7][8] The plaintiffs' framing: "one of the largest and most blatant ongoing thefts of intellectual property in history."[7]

So what: if your RAG or fine-tune corpus includes song lyrics scraped from public sites — even from a licensed lyrics API you re-cache — assume the discovery bar is now "produce the corpus" and pull the tables before your next model retrain, because the willful-infringement statutory damages here scale per work, not per model.

FSB Chair Andrew Bailey warns G20 that frontier AI is now a financial-stability concern — Financial Stability Board

In his letter to G20 Finance Ministers and Central Bank Governors ahead of their September meeting, Bank of England Governor Andrew Bailey — writing as chair of the FSB — flagged "increasingly sophisticated autonomy and problem-solving abilities" in frontier AI models as a threat category, with cybersecurity risk as the most immediate transmission channel and AI-linked equity valuations as a market-correction amplifier.[5] The recommended jurisdictional priorities: safe and responsible model release, financial-institution response and recovery capability, and resilience of critical third-party tech providers.[5]

So what: if you sell agentic tooling into a bank, insurer or asset manager, the RFP language you will see this quarter now maps to FSB-endorsed categories — bring a written incident-response runbook and a third-party-dependency chart to your next call, not a capabilities deck.

OpenAI to end model access to Cursor on November 12 after SpaceX's acquisition of Anysphere — OpenAI

OpenAI notified SpaceX on August 28 that it will wind down the custom agreement that provides OpenAI models to Cursor on November 12, 2026, following SpaceX's $60B acquisition of Cursor parent Anysphere earlier this month.[9][10] OpenAI framed the change-of-control call as an inability to be "confident" SpaceX will honour its terms of service, citing prior contract disputes with Musk-owned firms; Cursor CEO Michael Truell told CNBC OpenAI models handle roughly 5% of Cursor traffic, and Anthropic publicly moved to expand Claude support inside Cursor.[10]

So what: if your team standardised on Cursor + GPT-class models for editor-side agents, budget an eight-week migration to Claude Sonnet 5 or Gemini 2.5 defaults before November 12 — the change-of-control clause is the template every AI-vendor procurement lawyer will now add to next quarter's paper.

Anthropic opens a research preview of the Model Hardware Standard — Anthropic

Anthropic on August 27 opened a first research preview of the Model Hardware Standard (MHS), a model-agnostic specification for AI agents to operate physical lab and manufacturing hardware — microscopes, liquid handlers, robotic arms, laser-calibration rigs on quantum computers — in parallel, cutting typical device-integration work from weeks to hours.[11][12] The initial partners are HHMI Janelia Research Campus plus a small group of scientific labs and advanced manufacturers; the standard is planned to be open-sourced beyond the preview.[11][12]

So what: if you are already writing MCP servers for software tools, MHS is the same idea one layer down at the driver / bus / serial-protocol boundary — read the preview spec now so your 2027 lab or factory-floor integrations are one adaptor away from any frontier model, not one bespoke integration per vendor.
